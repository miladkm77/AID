Atmospheric Interface Design

A gas,
not glass.

Every button is a sealed chamber of noble gas. Your finger pushes it aside, it settles without a wave, and the colours behind it light it up.

Not glass. Not flat.

A volume, not a surface

No specular highlight, no lens. Depth comes from density: where the gas is thicker it scatters more and bends the background more.

Pressure under the finger

The gas thins under a touch and compresses around it. When you let go it settles back, over-damped, with no waves.

Content is the light

Every colour behind a container is a light source. A photo near a button tints the gas before it passes underneath.

Honest about physics

Each behaviour in the spec is labelled physical, approximation or artistic. No claim is made that the model does not earn.

Both phones are built only from <aid-scene>, <aid-button> and <aid-tabs>. On the left the content behind the gas is drawn on a canvas, so the full model runs. On the right it is real HTML, which is the mode a panel or a website uses. The gas is a volume, not a lens: it pools under its own weight, takes its colour from the content around it, and bends the background only where it is disturbed.

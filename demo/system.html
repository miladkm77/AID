Use it

One fixed scene serves a whole app or page: one GPU context, and only containers in view are simulated. It works the same in an installed PWA.

<script type="module" src="aid.js"></script>

<aid-scene fixed style="--aid-gas: xenon">
  <!-- your whole app -->
  <aid-button variant="primary"><aid-icon name="plus"></aid-icon> New case</aid-button>
  <aid-field><input placeholder="Search"></aid-field>
  <label><aid-switch checked></aid-switch> Notifications</label>
</aid-scene>

Up to 96 gas containers can be in view at once. On HTML pages the gas is drawn over the page, so content light and refraction are not available; settling, pressure, excitation and depth are.
